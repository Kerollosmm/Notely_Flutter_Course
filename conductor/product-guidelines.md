# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Friendly & Encouraging**: The tone should be casual, helpful, and approachable to foster a positive user experience.
- **Supportive**: Assist users without being overbearing, using language that guides rather than instructs.
- **Optimistic**: Focus on productivity and creativity, making note-taking feel like a rewarding activity.

## Visual Aesthetic
- **Modern & Vibrant**: High-energy design using bold accent colors (specifically a vibrant yellow primary) against clean backgrounds.
- **Clean & Spacious**: Generous use of white space and rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-full`) to create a friendly and accessible interface.
- **Dark Mode First**: While supporting both, the design should feel native and sleek in dark mode, utilizing deep background colors (`#23220f`, `#2f2e1a`) with high-contrast text.

## Design System (Inferred from UI)
- **Primary Color**: Vibrant Yellow (`#f9f506`) used for accents, active states, and primary actions.
- **Typography**: 
  - **Display**: 'Spline Sans' for headers and UI elements, providing a modern and geometric look.
  - **Body**: 'Inter' or 'Open Sans' for readability in longer text blocks.
- **Iconography**: `Material Symbols Outlined` with a focus on simple, recognizable shapes. Filled variations are used for active states.
- **Components**:
  - **Cards**: Soft shadows (`shadow-soft`), rounded corners, and subtle hover effects (`hover:shadow-md`).
  - **Navigation**: Floating bottom navigation bar with blurred background (`backdrop-blur-md`) for a modern, app-like feel.
  - **Buttons**: Pill-shaped buttons (`rounded-full`) with clear states (active, hover).
